 Distribution Turkey [O. Cetin, pers. comm.], Greece (Aegean islands, e.g. Agathonisi, Cyprus), Israel, Palestine (West Bank), Syria, N Iraq, N Iran, Jordan, Lebanon, Kuwait Egypt (N Sinai) Type locality: “Aegypto”; restricted to SW Turkey, areas around Selcuk and Ephesus, by ZINNER 1972. asianus: Syria, Jordan, Lebanon, Israel; Type locality: not specifically given. cypriacus: Cyprus zinneri: Type locality: "Laerma': Rhodos Island, southern Dodekanes archipelago, Greece, elevation 300 m a.s.l.
 Reproduction oviparous  
 Types Holotype: ZFMK 92945, formerly MAFR (Museum Adolphi Friderici Regis) Lectotype: SMF 18189 (formerly 7397a) [asianus] Holotype: NMW 15186:3 (fide Zinner 1972, but not listed in type catalogue by Tiedemann et al. 1994 nor Tiedemann & Grillitsch 1999) [cypriacus] Holotype: ZFMK 92945. adult male, collected by A. CATTANEO. 27 May 2005; paratypes: CATTANEO private collection, Roma [zinneri]

 Comment Synonymy: Dolichophis mesopotamicus was described in a short paper with few details, fuzzy photos, and no DNA data. We requested more data from the authors but did not get any and hence decided to keep it as a nomen dubium for the time being until more information becomes available. Subspecies: Dolichophis jugularis cypriacus (ZINNER 1972) was described in a thesis and is not generally recognized. A genetic lineage on Rhodos corresponds to Dolichophis jugularis zinneri (Javorčík et al. 2024). Hybridization: D. caspius and D. jugularis may have hybridized in the past (Javorčík et al. 2024). Distribution: Reported from Bulgaria, Romania, Albania, Yugoslavia, Hungary but unconfirmed. Not listed by ENGELMANN et al. (1993). For a map see Sindaco et al. 2013 and Bar et al. 2021. There seems to be one isolated record or population in Egypt (North Sinai, Baha El Din et al. 2012).  
 Etymology Apparently named after Latin jugulum = collarbone, throat. D. j. asianus was apparently named (in error) after “Asia” even though the subspecies doesn’t occur there.
