 Comment Elaphe longissima romana has been elevated to species status by Lenk & Wüster 1999 and is now called E. lineata. Type species: Coluber aesculapi LACEPEDE 1789 is the type species of the genus Zamenis WAGLER 1830 (fide WILLIAMS & WALLACH 1989: 161). Synonymy: Kaiser et al. 2013 considered the generic name Richardwellsus Hoser 2012 invalid and rejected its use instead of Zamenis. Habitat: partly arboreal (Harrington et al. 2018). Distribution: Not in Poland fide Geniez 2018, Salvi et al. 2018, but recorded from isolated localities there fide Kurek et al. 2017. For a map see Sindaco et al. 2013.  
 Etymology Named after the length of this species which is unusually large for European snakes. The genus was named after the Greek “za” = great, and “menos” = strength.
