 Reproduction oviparous (not imputed, fide Zimin et al. 2022)  
 Types Holotype: nondesignated, location unknown (fide WALLACH et al. 2014: 416) Holotype: nondesignated, location unknown (fide WALLACH et al. 2014: 416) [fuscus]  
 Diagnosis Diagnosis: Dorsal scales without keels; dorsal surface of snout with longitudinal concave furrow; frontal narrower than the supraocular; two loreals; no dark spot between parietals and angle of mouth.  Comment Venomous! CARRANZA et al. (2006) suggested to subdivide the species M. insignitus into M. insignitus fuscus and M. i. insignitus. Distribution: For a map see Sindaco et al. 2013 and Bar et al. 2021. For a map of subspecies see De Haan in Delachaux & Niestlé 1997.  
 Etymology Named after Latin insignis, conspicuous, manifest, eminent, notable, famous, distinguished, outstanding, and insignitus = marked, clear, plain. M. i. fuscus was named after Latin “fuscus”, meaning dark or dusky.
